## New findings

| Priority | Finding in simple words | Reproduced evidence | Required correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Supplier verification is enforced for AI advice but not final calculation | A synthetic imported supplier had default `recipient_type=company`, `recipient_type_verified_at=null` and known filer status. A company-specific rule calculated with `needsReview=0` and final approval returned 200. | Require confirmed recipient evidence in the engine and approval path, including manual and remembered categories. Filer status must not verify recipient type. |
| High | The historical schedule can calculate and approve unsupported years | A January 2027 entry calculated PKR 4,000 from PKR 100,000 using the supplied Finance Act 2024 table; approval returned 200. No effective-period selection/check exists. The general reference-basis notice does not enforce applicability. | Define supported effective dates from reviewed source data; reject other periods or keep explicitly separate hypothetical estimates ineligible for final approval. Do not assume calendar year equals Pakistan tax year. |
| High | Reconciliation ignores part-paid batches | A two-section batch recorded a synthetic PKR 15,000 CPR payment through the normal API. Annual reconciliation still rendered `Total WHT Paid PKR 0`, because it counts only wholly paid batches. | Sum recorded challan payments for the relevant period, and show partial-payment state. Keep drafts separate from committed liability. |
| Medium | Removing all income-tax entries leaves the old positive draft | After a PKR 4,000 draft was generated, its sole entry was reopened and approved with no withholding treatment. Recalculation returned 409 and the old PKR 4,000 draft/lines remained. Its approval correctly returned 409, limiting the risk, but it still appears in summaries. | Atomically clear or visibly invalidate an obsolete empty income draft, as the sales-tax engine already does. |
| Medium | An incomplete challan set cannot recover on retry | Starting from a genuine approved two-section synthetic batch, the probe simulated an interrupted generation by removing only its unpaid test section. Retry returned 200 with `created=1`; the missing section was not recreated. | Generate all sections atomically or upsert every expected missing section on retry while preserving recorded payments. |

Source locations:

- Recipient metadata is not selected/checked by `lib/whtEngine.ts` (vendor selection and lines 151–199); final approval checks only `needsReview` in `app/api/wht/batch/[batchId]/approve/route.ts`.
- Year input permits 2000–2100 in `app/api/wht/calc/route.ts:14`; neither engine selects an effective-dated schedule. Sales calculation has the same structural absence; the live out-of-period approval probe exercised income.
- `app/(dashboard)/company/[id]/recon/annual/page.tsx:98` counts paid batch totals instead of individual paid challans.
- `lib/whtEngine.ts:217` throws before clearing empty obsolete drafts.
- `app/api/wht/batch/[batchId]/challans/route.ts:103` returns as soon as any challan exists, and subsequent section inserts are separate writes.

## Scope and reproduction

The owner chose the supplied historical references. Numeric salary bands are still absent, so salary tax remains unavailable; tiered rent is explicitly unsupported. This audit does not establish current tax-law compliance or measured classification accuracy. There is no expert-labelled AI accuracy benchmark or proven time-saving percentage.

Run the local production server with a non-production database. The usual regression command is:

```text
npm run test:workflow
npx tsx tests/boundaries.integration.ts
npm run test:audit
npm run test:release
```

Additional probes, in order:

```text
npx tsx tests/reaudit-edges.integration.ts
npx tsx tests/reaudit-payments.integration.ts
```

These probes create synthetic users, vendors, transactions, batches and explicitly fake payment records. The second checks the first probe's synthetic company name before writing and simulates a partial-generation state only inside that fixture. They do not pay/file tax or alter the supplied workbooks. The expanded ledger suite imports the supplied ledger into an isolated test company and makes metered live AI calls. Private outputs remain under `/tmp/taxtech-reaudit-*`; never publish private fixture credentials or client ledger data.
